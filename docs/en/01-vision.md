# 01 · Vision and goals (EN)
**Problem.** Anyone with a new personal Google Play account must run a closed test with **12 testers for 14 continuous days** before applying for production access. Solo devs and vibecoders lose weeks hunting for testers, and many fail because people drop out midway.
**Proposal.** A **free, open-source** platform where devs form **synchronized 14-day rounds** and test each other's apps. Since everyone needs the same window at the same time, everyone has a reason to stay until the end.
**Goals.** (1) Ship a simple, working MVP in production, open to the community (required). (2) At least 2 complete rounds in the first 2 months. (3) North star: apps that finished a round and applied for production access. (4) Admin panel with metrics (P2, only after the MVP).
**Principles.** Simple before complete · transparency (code, docs, roadmap are open) · honesty (we never promise Google's approval) · bilingual · free at first.
**Not.** A paid-testers marketplace · APK hosting · approval guarantee · public review exchange.
