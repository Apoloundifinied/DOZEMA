# 03 · How it works (EN)
| Phase | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Queue | — | Dev joins with an app (owner) or without (helper) |
| Forming | instant | Hourly job creates a round when ≥ 13 owners are waiting |
| Setup | 48 h | Everyone joins groups and opts in to the others' tests |
| Testing | 14 days | Check-ins and feedback |
| Buffer | 2 days | Margin for late starters or failures |
| Finished | — | Reputation updated; each dev applies for production access in Play Console |
**Dev steps.** Create a closed test in Play Console and copy the opt-in link → create a Google Group and add its address to the testers list → register the app on Dozena and join the queue → approve group join requests and test the others' apps → apply for production access at the end.
**Tester steps (per app).** Join the group → opt in and install via Google Play → use the app and check in on ≥ 2 different days → write private feedback on at least 3 apps.
**Rules.** Nobody tests their own app · helpers get up to 5 apps (those with the fewest testers) · queue priority to people who need the rule, then arrival order, owners before helpers · dropouts lose reputation · no public Play Store reviews in exchange for tests · only Google Play links.
**What Dozena doesn't control.** Google's approval, its internal engagement checks, and rule changes. Always check the official Play Console docs.
